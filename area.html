Explore the Wye Valley

One of England's most beautiful corners, right on your doorstep — from riverside market towns to ancient woodland and rolling border country.

Your countryside basecamp

Perfectly placed for adventure & relaxation

Field Cottage sits on the edge of the Wye Valley Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, five minutes from Ross-on-Wye and within easy reach of the Forest of Dean, Hereford, the Brecon Beacons and the Welsh borders. Whether you want gentle riverside strolls, a day on the water, historic towns or family days out, it's all close by.

Ross-on-Wye

A charming market town just five minutes away, set above a sweeping bend in the River Wye. Independent shops, riverside walks, cafes and a lively weekly market.

Canoeing the River Wye

Take to the water on one of Britain's most scenic rivers. Gentle paddles and full-day descents launch from Ross, Symonds Yat and beyond.

Symonds Yat & the Rock

Famous viewpoints over the Wye gorge, woodland trails, a hand-pulled ferry and wild peregrine falcons soaring above the river.

Forest of Dean

Ancient woodland for walking, cycling and family adventures — including Go Ape, sculpture trails and the Puzzlewood film location.

Goodrich Castle

A magnificent medieval ruin a short drive away, with battlements to explore and far-reaching views across the valley.

Hereford & the Mappa Mundi

The cathedral city of Hereford, home to the medieval Mappa Mundi and Chained Library, is around 25 minutes away.

Hay-on-Wye

The world-famous "town of books" on the edge of the Brecon Beacons — packed with bookshops, galleries and cafes.

Monmouth & the borders

Cross into Wales to explore the historic town of Monmouth, Tintern Abbey and the dramatic lower Wye Valley.

Walks from the door

Quiet country lanes and footpaths begin right outside the cottage, with the long-distance Wye Valley Walk close by.

Getting here

Easy to reach, wonderfully tucked away

Despite its peaceful setting, Field Cottage is simple to get to. Junction of the M50 motorway is just a few minutes away, connecting to the M5 and the wider network.

  • Ross-on-Wye — 5 min
  • Hereford — 25 min
  • Forest of Dean — 20 min
  • Monmouth — 20 min
  • Cheltenham — 45 min
  • Hay-on-Wye — 50 min
