Welcome to Field Cottage

Country comfort, thoughtfully restored

Originally built in 1850 and lovingly refurbished in 2022, Field Cottage blends period character with everything a modern family or group of friends needs for a relaxed getaway. Tucked away in the village of Peterstow on the edge of the Wye Valley Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, it offers genuine peace and quiet — with the market town of Ross-on-Wye only a short drive away.

With four generous bedrooms, two living spaces, a well-equipped kitchen and a large secure garden, there's room for everyone to gather together or find a quiet corner of their own.
