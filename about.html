About & Rooms

A characterful country house refurbished for comfortable, modern living — with room for eight to relax together.

The cottage

A home from home in the Herefordshire countryside

Built in 1850 and fully refurbished in 2022, Field Cottage is a four-bedroom country house in the village of Peterstow, on the edge of the Wye Valley Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. It comfortably sleeps eight across four bedrooms, with two living spaces, a dining room and a well-equipped kitchen — all surrounded by open countryside and just five minutes from Ross-on-Wye.

Inside the cottage

Space to gather, room to relax

With two separate living rooms, there's always somewhere to be sociable around the fire or to slip away with a book. The dining room seats the whole group, and the kitchen is fully equipped for everything from a quick breakfast to a celebration dinner.

Throughout, you'll find a relaxed country style — natural materials, plenty of light, and country views from every window.
