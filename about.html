About & Rooms

A characterful country house and self-contained annexe in a peaceful Wye Valley lane — private, comfortable and surrounded by open countryside.

The cottage

A peaceful retreat in the heart of the Wye Valley

Field Cottage sits in a very quiet lane in the Peterstow countryside, near the market town of Ross-on-Wye in the Wye Valley Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. There are views of the countryside from every window, and the house is far enough from its neighbours to offer a real sense of privacy — yet it's well connected, around ten minutes' drive from Ross-on-Wye and twenty-five from Hereford.

You'll have the whole property to yourselves: a four-bedroom main house, a separate self-contained one-bedroom annexe with its own kitchen and living area, and the gardens. The cottage comfortably sleeps eight.

The cottage

Space to gather, room to relax

Built in 1850, the main house has four bedrooms upstairs and, downstairs, two separate living rooms, a dining room and a well-equipped kitchen — so there's always somewhere to be sociable or to slip away with a book.

Bed linen, sheets, towels, toilet roll, shampoo and shower gel are all included, so you can simply arrive and settle in.

  • Two living rooms
  • Dining room & kitchen
  • Dishwasher, washer & dryer
  • Travel cot & stair gate available

The annexe

A self-contained space of its own

About ten metres from the main house, the annexe is fully self-contained. Downstairs there's a double bedroom with no stairs to navigate and its own accessible shower room — ideal for anyone who'd prefer to avoid steps.

Upstairs is a light-filled, open-plan living room and kitchen with a dining table, crowned by a vaulted ceiling and apex glazing that frames the fields beyond. It has its own internet, too.

  • Ground-floor double bedroom
  • Accessible shower room
  • Open-plan living room & kitchen
  • Its own internet
The gardens

Secure, private & full of country views

The gardens are enclosed with fencing, hedges and a gate — a safe spot for children to play and for dogs to roam. There's a large lawn in front of the main house and another by the annexe, with shrubs and flower beds to enjoy through the seasons and views over the surrounding fields, farmland and orchards.

  • Fully fenced & gated
  • Two lawns & outdoor furniture
  • Free parking for 3–4 cars
  • EV charging point (adapter required)

Good to know

Booking & house information

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 4pm, check-out by 10am. Self check-in. Earlier check-in or later check-out is sometimes possible if there's no booking either side — just ask and we'll do our best.

Pets & children

Children and well-behaved dogs are warmly welcome, with a secure, fully fenced garden. Assistance animals are always welcome.

House rules

No smoking inside. Events or parties only by prior arrangement. Supermarket deliveries (Sainsbury's & Asda) can be sent straight to the cottage for your arrival.

Accessibility

The annexe has a step-free double bedroom and accessible wet-room shower. We're happy to help where we can in a period property — see our accessibility statement for full details.
